# ADR-001

## Title

Engineering Platform is AI Provider Independent

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The Auto Responder engineering environment must not depend on a specific AI provider.

The engineering system should remain usable with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, or any future AI assistant.

## Decision

The engineering platform is built around documentation, standards, processes, skills, and knowledge.

AI assistants are considered interchangeable engineering workers.

## Consequences

- Easier migration between AI providers.
- Better maintainability.
- Long-term sustainability.
